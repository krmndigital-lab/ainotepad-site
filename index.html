AI Notepad

Handwriting & PDF notes for Android · by KRMN Digital

Type, write by hand or bring in a PDF: AI Notepad keeps typed text and handwriting on the same page, turns handwriting into text, finds words in your handwriting and pictures, and summarizes long notes. The AI runs on your phone. No account, no upload: your notes stay on your device and in the cloud storage you choose.

What it does

  • Write with a stylus or your finger: pens, fountain pen, pencil, marker, highlighter, eraser, lasso, shapes that straighten themselves.
  • Type rich text with headings, lists, check boxes, colours and links, next to your handwriting.
  • AI on the device: handwriting to text, search in handwriting and pictures, summary and key points, title and tag suggestions, tidy formatting, action items and reminder dates, math from handwriting, translation.
  • Import PDFs to write on, pictures, Word, text and Markdown files, Google Keep and Evernote exports; share anything to the app.
  • Voice notes recorded while you write.
  • Organize with folders, tags, favourites, colours, note lock and reminders.
  • Back up and sync to your own Google Drive, Dropbox, Yandex Disk, OneDrive, WebDAV (Nextcloud) or a folder, with optional end-to-end encryption.
  • Export PDF with selectable text, images, Word, Markdown and plain text. 20 languages.
