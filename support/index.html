Frequently asked

Where are my notes? On your device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded unless you connect your own cloud storage in Settings → Backup and sync, or share a note yourself.

How do I move my notes to a new phone? Connect the same cloud storage on the new phone and choose Restore, or make a backup file on the old phone (Settings → Backup and sync → Backup file) and open it on the new one.

Handwriting is not turned into text. Choose your writing language in Settings → Handwriting languages; the language model (about 20 MB) is downloaded once. Write on lines if you can: straight lines are recognized best.

My finger draws instead of scrolling (or the other way round). Tap the hand icon in the pen bar: with a stylus you usually want fingers to scroll and the pen to write.

I forgot the passphrase of my encrypted backup. It cannot be recovered: the copies in your cloud are encrypted with it. The notes on your device are not affected; set a new passphrase and back up again.

I bought Pro on another device. Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account.
